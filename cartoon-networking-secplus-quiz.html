Passing score: 750 out of 900 (≈83%)

MCQ questions: All-or-nothing. No partial credit.

PBQ (drag-drop/matching): May award partial credit — this quiz gives 0.5 pts for ≥50% correct.

❌ The "name credit" myth: 100% FALSE. CompTIA does NOT give points for writing your name. Ignore that rumor completely!

Unanswered = wrong. Always attempt every question — no penalty for guessing.
