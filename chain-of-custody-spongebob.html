🔐 Evidence Log #BB-001

Chain of Custody
🌊 Bikini Bottom Edition 🌊

The Krabby Patty Secret Formula is the evidence. Don't let Plankton tamper with it.

1
🥜
Collection
🔬 Sandy Cheeks — First Responder
Sandy discovers the Secret Formula at a crime scene and seals it in an evidence bag. She is the very first link in the chain — and every link matters. Nothing is touched without documentation.
📋 Who touched it first?
2
🧽
Transfer
🧽 SpongeBob — Recipient
Sandy hands the formula to SpongeBob, who signs the log: "I, SpongeBob, received this formula at 3:00 PM." Every single handoff gets dated, timed, and signed. No exceptions, no shortcuts.
✍️ Signed, sealed, delivered
3
🦀
Storage
💰 Mr. Krabs' Safe — Secure Custody
SpongeBob locks the formula in Mr. Krabs' safe. Access is logged every time the safe is opened. Plankton can't just waltz in and touch it — all access requires authorization and documentation.
🔒 Access control is everything
4
🔍
Integrity
⚖️ Integrity Check — Tamper Evidence
The formula must look identical throughout the entire process. If the bag is opened, the seal is broken, or anything looks different — that's a red flag defense lawyers will pounce on in court.
🔏 Same in, same out
5
🎤
Testimony
🏛️ Courtroom — The Final Link
In court, everyone in the chain — Sandy → SpongeBob → Mr. Krabs — must testify that they handled the evidence properly and did not tamper with it. Every person is accountable for their link in the chain.
🗣️ Every link testifies
🚨 What Breaks the Chain? 🚨
🐌
Gap in Chain
Gary the snail is found holding the formula with no record of how he got it. An unexplained gap in the chain means the evidence can be challenged — where was it? Who had it?
⭐
Integrity Compromised
Patrick was alone with the formula for an hour and can't remember what he did with it. Unaccounted time with evidence = integrity destroyed. The defense will have a field day.
🎸
Disputed Transfer
The log says Squidward signed for the formula — but Squidward says he never touched it. Conflicting records poison the entire chain. If signatures don't match reality, nothing is trustworthy.
